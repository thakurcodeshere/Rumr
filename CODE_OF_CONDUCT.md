# RUMR Code of Conduct & Contributor Covenant

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, welcoming, and intellectually stimulating environment, we as contributors and maintainers of **RUMR** pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We believe that **intellectual friction and contrarian debate are healthy**, but personal targeting, slander, and bad-faith harassment destroy honest discourse.

---

## The Rumr Protocol Standards

### 1. Topic-First Principle & Radical Candor
- **Focus on the Argument, Not the Person**: Debate the validity of ideas, systems, economic models, and societal patterns. Direct personal insults (*ad-hominem* attacks) are strictly prohibited.
- **The ≤ 3-Word Hard Constraint**: Topics created within the ecosystem must strictly adhere to 3 words or fewer. Attempting to bypass this constraint to insert personally identifiable information (PII) or slander is a direct violation.
- **Respect Anonymity Boundaries**: Never attempt to de-anonymize, doxx, or reverse-engineer a participant's identity outside of the authorized 3-layer progressive mutual consent protocol.

### 2. Positive Behaviors That Build Community
- Constructive engagement in topic debate rooms and encrypted tunnels.
- Demonstrating empathy toward different lived experiences and contrarian viewpoints.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and open discourse.
- Protecting the cryptographic security and privacy of fellow participants.

### 3. Unacceptable Behaviors
- **Targeted Harassment & Bullying**: Stalking, intimidation, or persistent unwanted contact after a participant has declined conversation.
- **Doxxing & Identity Leakage**: Publishing private data (such as real names, physical addresses, phone numbers, or private photos) without explicit cryptographic authorization.
- **Hate Speech & Discrimination**: Attacks based on race, gender, sexual orientation, religion, disability, or nationality.
- **Commercial Bot Spam & Sybil Swarms**: Deploying automated bots to manipulate topic heat scores or spam encrypted tunnels.
- **Non-Consensual Identity Demands**: Coercing participants into premature layer reveals before mutual trust is established.

## Enforcement Guidelines & Responsibilities

Community leaders and maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

### 1. Correction
- **Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language, minor boundary crossing, or topic formatting violations.
- **Consequence**: A private, written warning with an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. The offending topic node will be purged from the resonance matrix.

### 2. Warning
- **Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions demonstrating disrespect or harassment.
- **Consequence**: Temporary suspension of topic creation privileges for 48 hours.

### 3. Temporary Ban
- **Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including persistent harassment or attempting to bypass linguistic constraints.
- **Consequence**: A temporary ban from all topic broadcast stages, audio rooms, and encrypted tunnels for 14 days.

### 4. Permanent Termination
- **Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation, including doxxing, hate speech, severe harassment, or malicious platform exploitation.
- **Consequence**: Permanent termination of phone-verified account and blacklisting of associated cryptographic public keys.

## Reporting & Incident Response

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported directly to the Rumr Security & Trust Operations team at:

📧 **conduct@rumr.network** or **security@rumr.network**

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. The maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.
